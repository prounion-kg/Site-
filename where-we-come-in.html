Client situations

Where we come in

International companies usually reach us at a specific moment. These are the situations we handle most often.

You need people here before your company exists.

We hire your first employees through Pro Union LLC under Employer of Record, so work can start while your entity is being registered. Once it is ready, we transfer the team to your company.

Employer of Record

You want to test the market before committing.

We assess the regulatory and commercial landscape, arrange business missions and introduce you to potential partners and clients. If you decide to proceed, a small local team can start under Employer of Record without the cost of setting up a company.

Market Entry

You work with several local providers, and nobody owns the result.

We replace a chain of lawyers, recruiters and agents with one team and a dedicated manager. You receive one line of communication and one accountable partner for your presence in Kyrgyzstan.

How We Work

You have a registered company, but no partners or clients yet.

We identify and introduce relevant partners, clients and suppliers in the public and private sectors, and support you through negotiations until agreements are in place.

Business Development

Your local team is growing faster than your HR function.

We take over HR administration, payroll and compliance fully or partially, so growth does not create gaps in documentation or labour risk.

HR & Employer of Record

HR Compliance Health Check
